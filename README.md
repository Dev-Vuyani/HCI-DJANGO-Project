Study-Buddy is a centralized academic productivity and wellness app built using Django. 
This project was developed as part of a research assignment to identify and address the mental load students experience from managing heavy academic responsibilities.

🎯 Project Purpose
Many students are overwhelmed by multiple academic deadlines, assignments, and study schedules—all of which can affect their mental well-being and academic performance.
Study-Buddy is designed to help students balance their academic workload while taking care of their mental health through synced reminders and wellness suggestions.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Backend: Django (Python)
Database: SQLite (default Django setup)
Frontend: Django Templates (HTML/CSS/JS)

✨ Features
✅ Study Plan Creator – Organize and schedule study sessions with customizable durations.

✅ Daily Task Manager – Create daily academic tasks to stay on track with assignments and projects.

✅ Deadline Tracker – Add and manage important submission dates with reminders.

✅ Wellness Checks – Timely alerts to take breaks and avoid burnout (e.g., “Let’s take a 10-minute walk”).

✅ Study Motivation Alerts – Periodic motivational quotes and tips to keep students inspired.
